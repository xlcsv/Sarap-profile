THE MESSAGEFLOW G · AYOS BA?

How are you?

The song is a reminder to check in on the people who matter to us. It is also about gratitude, getting through hard times, and not rushing through life.

01

Check in

Asking how someone is doing matters most when we take time to listen and let them know they are not alone.

02

Look around

Some people keep going and find a reason to smile through difficult times. Remembering that can help us appreciate what we have.

03

Take your time

Focus on what you truly need, make room for what brings you joy, and be patient—some things take time.

“

Sometimes, showing you care starts with one simple question.
